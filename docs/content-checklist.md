## Corrected from the previous site
- **"Listed among community partners of the EYRND Ontario Health Team" — removed.** In the Jan 2025 OHT deck, Island Breeze appears on a **Carefirst** slide listing Carefirst's partners. The OHT itself is also listed there as a Carefirst partner. The site now names Carefirst as the collaborator.
- **Unit 314 removed from visitor information and structured data.** It is the registered office in the corporate record, and two directors list it as their own address, so it may be a private residence. The site asks visitors to call for where to meet.
- **"Every Monday" removed.** The most recent source is from 2025, so there's no current confirmation.
- **Markham Museum** is now under "Places we've visited", not partners.
- **The Black Health Initiative card was removed** (see below).
