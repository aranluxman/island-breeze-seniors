About us

A seniors day program in Markham built on friendship

More than just activities… it’s a healthier, happier you!

Island Breeze Seniors Day Program Ltd. is a federally incorporated not-for-profit organization, founded in October 2018 and based at Hagerman Corners Community Homes in Markham. We serve adults aged 55 and older — and the caregivers who love them — with senior activities in Markham that help people connect, engage and socialize. Think of us as a community adult day program in York Region — with a Caribbean heart.

We are governed by a volunteer board of directors and run with the help of dedicated volunteers and community facilitators.

  • Social

    Friendship, conversation and a place where everyone knows your name.

  • Cognitive

    Games, crafts and learning that keep the mind sharp.

  • Physical

    Wellness workshops and gentle activity for a healthier body.

  • Cultural

    A warm, Caribbean-inspired seniors program in the Greater Toronto Area that celebrates who you are.

What we do

Senior activities & social programs in Markham

Our calendar changes week to week with new workshops and events. Please call 416-319-7763 to check what’s on this week.

  • Weekly crochet class

    At Hagerman Corners Community Homes, facilitated by one of our volunteers. Beginners welcome.

    Every Monday

  • Domino game nights

    Dominoes and friendly competition — the group always looks forward to the interaction and socializing.

  • Arts & crafts

    Creative, hands-on sessions to make something beautiful together.

  • Laughter Yoga

    A joyful wellness workshop for seniors that combines laughter, breathing and gentle movement.

  • Meditation & wellness

    Calming sessions focused on stress relief, balance and whole-person wellness.

  • Healing Through Poetry

    A supportive workshop for seniors in South Markham exploring grief and healing through the written word.

  • Community outings

    Group trips to local spots — like our visit to the Markham Museum’s older adults program.

  • Special events

    Tea parties, health fairs and seasonal celebrations with our community partners.
