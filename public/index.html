Common questions

Who can join?

Island Breeze describes its programs as serving adults aged 55 and older in Markham. If you're not sure whether a program is right for you or someone you care for, please give us a call.

How do I register?

Call 416-319-7763 and we'll talk you through it. You can also send us a message and ask for a call back.

What does it cost?

Costs can vary by activity. Please call us to ask about the activity you're interested in.

Can a caregiver attend?

Our mission includes older adults and their caregivers. Please call to talk about coming together, so we can plan the visit with you.

What are the current program times?

Our schedule changes through the year. Please call 416-319-7763 for this month's activities and times.

What accessibility or transportation arrangements are available?

Please call before your visit so we can talk through access, mobility needs and how you'll get there.

About Island Breeze

A community built on friendship

Island Breeze Seniors Day Program is a not-for-profit organization run by a volunteer board with the help of volunteers and community facilitators. We bring older adults together to stay active, stay connected and enjoy life — and we welcome the caregivers who support them.

Our roots are Caribbean, and so is our welcome: warm, friendly and full of good conversation.

Community stories & milestones

Our story so far

A few moments from our history, each linked to its public source.

  1. October 2018

    Incorporated as a not-for-profit

    Island Breeze Seniors Day Program Ltd. was incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act.

    Source: Corporations Canada record (opens in a new tab)

  2. 2021–2022

    Guiding Light

    Received a $4,000 federal grant for the Guiding Light project through the Supporting Black Communities stream (September 2021 to September 2022).

    Source: Government of Canada grants record (opens in a new tab)

  3. 2022–2023

    Moment In Time

    Received an $18,750 New Horizons for Seniors Program grant for the Moment In Time project (March 2022 to March 2023).

    Source: Government of Canada grants record (opens in a new tab)

  4. February 2024

    Markham Museum visit

    Members visited the Markham Museum's older adults program.

    Source: Island Breeze on Instagram (opens in a new tab)

  5. 2025

    Part of Carefirst's partner network

    Carefirst Seniors and Community Services Association listed Island Breeze among its social care and community support partners in presentations in January and July 2025.

    Source: HSPN webinar slides, July 22, 2025 (opens in a new tab)

  6. 2026

    Cultural Arts for Holistic Healing

    A United Way–funded workshop series for seniors in South Markham, closing with a celebration luncheon in July.

    Source: Island Breeze on Facebook (opens in a new tab)
