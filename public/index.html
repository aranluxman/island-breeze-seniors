About us

More than just activities… it’s a healthier, happier you!

Island Breeze Seniors Day Program Ltd. is a federally incorporated not-for-profit organization, founded in October 2018 and based at Hagerman Corners Community Homes in Markham. We serve adults aged 55 and older — and the caregivers who love them — with programs that help people connect, engage and socialize.

We are governed by a volunteer board of directors and run with the help of dedicated volunteers and community facilitators.

Our mission

“To be the premier day center for older adults and their caregivers, where our clients can connect and engage.”

We provide a stimulating cultural environment that helps maintain social, cognitive and physical well-being, while supporting equal access to community resources.

Social

Friendship, conversation and a place where everyone knows your name.

Cognitive

Games, crafts and learning that keep the mind sharp.

Physical

Wellness workshops and gentle activity for a healthier body.

Cultural

A warm, Caribbean-inspired space that celebrates who you are.

What we do

Programs & activities

Our calendar changes week to week with new workshops and events. Please call ahead to check what’s on.

🧶

Weekly crochet class

Every Monday at Hagerman Corners Community Homes, facilitated by one of our volunteers. Beginners welcome.

🎲

Game nights

Dominoes and friendly competition — the group always looks forward to the interaction and socializing.

🎨

Arts & crafts

Creative, hands-on sessions to make something beautiful together.

😄

Laughter Yoga

A joyful wellness workshop for seniors that combines laughter, breathing and gentle movement.

🧘

Meditation & holistic wellness

Calming sessions focused on stress relief, balance and whole-person wellness.

✍️

Healing Through Poetry

A supportive workshop for seniors in South Markham exploring grief and healing through the written word.

🚌

Community outings

Group trips and excursions to local spots — like our visit to the Markham Museum’s older adults program.

🫖

Special events

Tea parties, health fairs and seasonal celebrations with our community partners.

Stronger together

In the community

Carefirst

Carefirst has hosted a tea party for our seniors, and our members joined the Carefirst Health Fair at the YMCA in Markham.

Black Health Initiative

During Black History Month 2025 we launched a Black Health initiative to support the health and well-being of Black older adults in our community.

Eastern York Region North Durham OHT

Island Breeze is listed among the community partners of the Eastern York Region North Durham Ontario Health Team.

Government of Canada

Our programs have been supported by federal community grants, including funding through the Supporting Black Communities stream of Employment and Social Development Canada.

Markham Museum

Our seniors have visited the Markham Museum’s older adults program.
