Recent project · 2026

Cultural Arts for Holistic Healing of Mind, Body and Spirit

A workshop series for seniors in South Markham, facilitated by Island Breeze with ACSA Community Services and supported by United Way Greater Toronto. From January to July, members drummed, wrote, danced, sculpted and celebrated together.

  1. JanDrumming for Wellness Rhythm and connection to start the year.
  2. FebHealing Grief Through Poetry A four-week series for reflection, healing and self-expression.
  3. MarAfro-Peruvian dance workshop Culture celebrated through dance, colour and joy.
  4. AprArt clay workshop Hands-on creativity with clay.
  5. MayHealth & wellness workshop Healthy-living information, activities and blood pressure checks.
  6. JunZumba, merengue & bingo A day of movement, music, laughter and games.
  7. JulFashion show & celebration lunch Closing the series with style, pride and culture.

Common questions

Who can join?

Island Breeze describes its programs as serving adults aged 55 and older in Markham. If you're not sure whether a program is right for you or someone you care for, call 416-319-7763 or email islandbreezeseniors@gmail.com.

How do I register?

Call 416-319-7763 or email islandbreezeseniors@gmail.com and we'll talk you through it. You can also send us a message below and ask for a call back.

Where are programs held?

In the Amenity Room at Hagerman Corners Community Homes, 4460 14th Avenue, Markham. Some events meet elsewhere (for example, Seniors Circle on November 18 is at Milliken Church, 3 Clayton Drive), and the calendar always shows the location. Get directions (opens Google Maps in a new tab).

What does it cost?

Costs can vary by activity. Call 416-319-7763 or email islandbreezeseniors@gmail.com to ask about the activity you're interested in.

Can a caregiver attend?

Our mission includes older adults and their caregivers in Markham. Call 416-319-7763 or email islandbreezeseniors@gmail.com to talk about coming together, so we can plan the visit with you.

When are the programs?

In November: Exercise Class every Monday (2–3 p.m.) and Chair Yoga every Tuesday (1–2 p.m.), plus a special event on Wednesdays. Every date and time is in this month’s calendar. Questions? Call Charmaine at 416-319-7763.

What accessibility or transportation arrangements are available?

Please call 416-319-7763 or email islandbreezeseniors@gmail.com before your visit so we can talk through access, mobility needs and how you'll get there.

Who runs Island Breeze?

Island Breeze was founded by Charmaine. It’s a not-for-profit run by a volunteer board, with the help of volunteers and community facilitators. For program information, call Charmaine at 416-319-7763 or email islandbreezeseniors@gmail.com.

About Island Breeze

A community built on friendship

Island Breeze Seniors Day Program is a not-for-profit organization run by a volunteer board with the help of volunteers and community facilitators. Our social programs for seniors in Markham and York Region bring older adults together to stay active, stay connected and enjoy life — and we welcome the caregivers who support them.

Our roots are Caribbean, and so is our welcome: warm, friendly and full of good conversation. We’re a Caribbean-rooted seniors program in the Greater Toronto Area, open to adults 55 and older.

Island Breeze was founded by Charmaine, who is also the person to call for program information: 416-319-7763.

Community stories & milestones

Our story so far

A few moments from our history.

  1. October 2018

    Incorporated as a not-for-profit

    Island Breeze Seniors Day Program was founded by Charmaine and incorporated as a not-for-profit under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act.

  2. February 2024

    Markham Museum visit

    Members visited the Markham Museum's older adults program.

  3. 2025

    Part of Carefirst's partner network

    Carefirst Seniors and Community Services Association listed Island Breeze among its social care and community support partners in presentations in January and July 2025.

  4. 2026

    Cultural Arts for Holistic Healing

    A workshop series for seniors in South Markham with drumming, poetry, dance, clay, wellness and a closing fashion show and celebration luncheon in July.

  5. November 2026

    Weekly programs at Hagerman Corners

    Exercise Class on Mondays and Chair Yoga on Tuesdays in the Amenity Room, with a special event most Wednesdays: a pancake breakfast, a Price Is Right game, Seniors Circle and bingo.
